Period 0 · 1534 → 1540 · De-facto establishment 1534-1540 · Counts' Feud emergency-coinage cascade + 1537 Joachimsdaler Copenhagen 14½-Lod anchor — mintmaster Reynold Junge
Pre-Møntordning period: the 14½-Lod Daler standard de facto established through Reynold Junge's coinage. Wilcke 1950 ch. 3 p. 242, table: «1534–39 · Jochimsdaler · 8 · 14½ Lod · 906,25»[209]. Three internal sub-periods: (1) 1534-1536 Counts' Feud emergency-coinage cascade — 2-Mark Klippinge at 10/8/7 Lod (.625/.500/.4375), 4-Skilling Klippinge at 5/4/3½ Lod (.3125/.250/.219), sub-Skilling tiers at 3-2½ Lod — wartime debasement of the lower-denomination tiers, without altering the Hauptkurant standard (Junge moves from Aarhus 1535 to Roskilde 1535-37 — mintmaster movement tracks the civil-war front). (2) 1537 Joachimsdaler anchor Copenhagen — 8 pieces/mark rough at 14½ Lod = 29.232 g rough / 26.494 g fine (Bruun-4276 + 4277 + 4278 three Stack's Bowers lots ≈ 28.5-28.94 g brutto). Metrically identical to Period A1 from 1541 — the 1537 coinage establishes the Daler standard de facto four years before the formal Møntordning. (3) 1538-1540 consolidation transition — sub-Skilling emergency coinage continues, but Junge prepares the central Copenhagen royal mint for the Møntordning programme; upon his exposure as fraudster 1540-41 hand-over to Povel Fechtel. Reference specimens: Bruun-4155 2 Mark Klippe 1535 Aarhus (Galster 92B, 11.05 g, variant 2 at 8 Lod), Bruun-4156 8 Skilling Klippe 1535 Aarhus (Galster 93, 2.72 g), Bruun-4194 1 Mark 1535 Roskilde, Bruun-4232 8 Skilling 1535 Stockholm (Sieg-24), Bruun-4276/4277/4278 Joachimsdaler 1537 Copenhagen, Bruun-4279 ½ Joachimsdaler 1537.dk Galster-Hede catalogue (continuous listing), Wilcke 1950 p. 242 (table), and Reynold Junge mintmaster continuity 1534-1540.

Period A1 · 1541 → 1543 · Baseline 1541-1543 · mf 8.827, 14½ Lod (906¼ ‰), 26.494 g fine per Daler — København
Baseline period of Christian III's Møntordning of 20 September 1541[207]: the mark at 24 per rough Cologne mark at 14½ Lod, i.e. eight daler of three marks each, Marken-fin mf 8.827, 26.494 g fine per Daler. Three-tier denomination: full-value Kurantmünze (Mark / 8 Skilling / 4 Skilling) at 14½ Lod; Scheidemünze (1 Skilling) at 4 Lod (250 ‰); billon (Hvid 4 Lod, Penning 2 Lod). Principal mint København under mintmaster Povel Fechtel.

Period A2 · 1544 → 1559 · 1544-1555 · ordinance of 27 September 1544 · mark at 13½ Lod — København
The ordinance of 27 September 1544 lowers the mark, at unchanged rough weight, to 13½ Lod (3 marks = 24.663 g fine; mf 9.481 in mark pieces), while the Gyldengroschen stays at 8 per mark, 14½ Lod (26.491 g fine)[208][211][212]. Economic background: northern-European silver price uptrend from 1544 onwards and Christian III's need for mint money to fund Schmalkaldic War subsidies.
