Full Krone-Mønt series under the Møntloven af 23. maj 1873: gold 10 / 20 Kroner (.900), silver Kurant 1 / 2 Kroner (.800), silver Scheide 25 / 10 øre (.600 / .400), bronze 5 / 2 / 1 øre — all denominations from the same coinage standard on a uniform accounting system (100 øre = 1 Krone). Tariff against the Reichsmark fixed at 8 Kroner = 9 Mark; gold convertibility suspended by Danmarks Nationalbank on 2 August 1914.
