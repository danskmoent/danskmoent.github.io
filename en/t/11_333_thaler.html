Period I · 15./31. Juli 1726 → 5. Januar 1813 · Kurantmøntfod in the Danish-Norwegian composite state · main everyday currency
Established by Friedrich IV's Forordning of 15/31 July 1726: 11⅓ Rigsdaler Courant from 1 fine Cologne mark — fine weight ≈ 20.63 g per daler. Principal vehicle of the Danish-Norwegian money supply through most of the 18th century (Friedrich IV, Christian VI, Friedrich V, Christian VII, Friedrich VI). In the Kingdom of Denmark it ends with the Statsbankerot of 5 January 1813 (Forordning of Friedrich VI); in Holstein the kurant recedes behind the Speciedaler from the Schimmelmann reform of 1788 (Speciesbank) onward, but it is not abolished and is still struck at Altona. Old stocks from 1726–1813 remained in circulation as petty coinage for decades after 1813, until the Krone reform of 1875.
