Phase A1 · 1524 → 1531 · Main phase 1524-1531 · 8 Sølvgylden/M at 14 Lod, mf 9.143, 25.578 g fine per Sølvgylden — Copenhagen + Malmø
Baseline phase of the Møntordning of 25 February 1524. Coinage begins with the 14-Penning piece (separate act of 26 February 1524) as Klipping redemption coin: Christian II's Klippinge are exchanged for 14-Penning pieces. Numista records f1g-* attest 14 Penning / 14 Penny from Copenhagen 1524 (Frederik I); mintmaster Jørgen Koch at Malmø, Hans Dringenberg at Aalborg (temporary branch). 4 Skilling 1525 Copenhagen (Galster f1g reference series). Sølvgylden coinage first documented from 1531 — likely pre-1531 Sølvgylden pieces within Wilcke's reach, but specific Galster datings begin in 1531. Main mint: Hovedmønten Malmø under mintmaster Jørgen Koch (1520s).

Phase A2 · 1532 → 1532 · Final consolidation 1532 · Galster 47-52 Sølvgylden series · Copenhagen
Frederik I's final coinage period, dominated by the Galster 47-52 Sølvgylden series: 1 Sølvgylden 1532 (Galster 47, 37.75 g brutto), 1½ Sølvgylden 1532 (Galster 48, 45.58 g — double piece), ½ Sølvgylden 1532 with Pillars/Statues obverse (Galster 48, 14.98 g), ¼ Sølvgylden 1532 (Galster 52, 7 g). Coinage at Copenhagen under mintmaster Jørgen Drews (transferred from Husum) in «Nødens Aaret 1532» (Wilcke 7-2 p. 187 verbatim) — Frederik I's last full year of coinage. King's death 10 April 1533; coinage pause until Christian III's coronation 1537 + de-facto establishment 1534-1540 8-Daler-Fod Phase 0 1534-1540.
