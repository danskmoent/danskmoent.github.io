Phase 0 · 1496 → 1513 · after the model of the Dutch great gold real
The heavy gold piece Hans strikes in 1496 (Malmø/Copenhagen) and 1502 (Malmø) (Galster 24)[1]. The piece count follows the Dutch great gold real of 1487, 16½ pieces to the troy mark[3]. The fineness of these pre-1514 Nobel is not independently attested[3].

Phase I · 1514 → 1532 · Møntordning Christian II 1514
The Møntordning Christian II of Summer 1514 (Malmø) specifies the Nobel: 16 pieces per Cologne mark at 23½ carats[4]. Attested by Christian II 1516 and 1518 Malmø (Galster 37) and Frederik I 1532 Copenhagen/Malmø (Galster 45) + undated Ribe (Galster 68 and 69); whether the Nobel of Christian II meet the fineness the Møntordning requires is, per Galster, uncertain[3].
