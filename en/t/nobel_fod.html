Phase I · 1514 → 1532 · Møntordning Christian II 1514 / Kongebrev Frederik I 1524
The Møntordning Christian II of Summer 1514 (Malmø) specifies the Nobel: 16 pieces per Cologne mark at 23½ carats[4]. Whether the Nobel of 1516 and 1518 meet the fineness required by the Møntordning of 1514 is unknown[18]. The letter of the Council of the Realm (Rigsraadets Brev) of 25 February 1524 sets 23 carats for the Nobel at the same 16 pieces[6]. This ordinance had «no practical significance»[7]; no coinage under it is attested. The Kongebrev of Frederik I of the same year 1524 restores the previous fineness of 23½ carats[9][8].
