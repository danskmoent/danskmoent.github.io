Phase I · 1514 → 1532 · de-jure standard, Christian II Møntordning 1514 (1514-1532)
The Christian II Møntordning of Summer 1514 (Malmø) codifies the Nobel de jure: 16 pieces per Cologne mark at 23½ carats[4]. Attested by Christian II 1516 + 1518 Malmø (Galster 37) and Frederik I 1532 Copenhagen/Malmø (Galster 45) + undated Ribe (Galster 68 + 69); the dated 1532 Nobler carry the 23½-carat fineness of the 1514 specification[5].
