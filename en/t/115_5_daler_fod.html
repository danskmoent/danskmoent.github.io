Phase I · 1604 → 1605 · Gold daler klippen to the ordinance of 8 September 1602
Square gold klippen of Christian IV, struck at Copenhagen in 1604 and continued in 1605. The ordinance of 8 September 1602 sets the minting formulas for the 8, 6 and 4 daler pieces — each denomination with its own carat and its own count per mark; the 3 daler is not in the ordinance and was struck, per the mint accounts, as half a 6-daler klippe[32]. The «daler» struck on them is a statutory face value in the silver unit of account, not an arbitrary one: it follows the gold-to-silver ratio Wilcke computes for the 4-daler klippe at 12.80 to 1, and it was reset when that ratio moved[39]. Circulation money they were not — Wilcke counts the gold of this ordinance among «Skuemønt til festlig Lejlighed»[55].
