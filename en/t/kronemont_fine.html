Phase I · 1693 → 1771 · Christian V → Christian VII · Copenhagen royal mint · «Fine Crown»
Sole phase of the Danish «Fine Crown»: per Christian V's Reskript of 31 December 1692, 13 Specieskronen are struck from 1 fine Cologne mark — fine weight ≈ 14.98 g per Krone (versus 14.964 g in the Glückstadt 10½-Krone-Fuß). Struck exclusively at the Copenhagen royal mint 1693–1771 under the mintmasters of the respective regents (Christian V to 1699, Friedrich IV 1699–1730, Christian VI 1730–1746, Friedrich V 1746–1766, Christian VII 1766–1808). The ⅔-Speciedaler tariff is carried through unchanged. Older stocks remained in circulation until the Statsbankerot of 5 January 1813.
