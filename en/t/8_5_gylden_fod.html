Phase A1 · 1514 → 1517 · Lovkompleks baseline 1514-1517 · 14 Lod (875 ‰), 27.51 g rough / 24.07 g fine per Sølvgylden — Malmø
Precursor acts 1513 (NOT part of the Lovkompleks itself): 22 July 1513 Haandfæstning (Wilcke 7-2 p. 4 verbatim «Den 22. Juli 1513 udstedtes Kong Christian II.s Haandfæstning») establishes the requirement «thue mark giør fyldest for en riksdlr.»; 1 August 1513 Dynes Blicher Beskikkelsesbrev appoints Diens (Hans) Blicher as mintmaster at Malmø — Skilling-Hvid tariff per Wilcke 7-2 p. 150 (Skilling 96/M at 5¾ Lod, Hvid 222/M at 4½ Lod). These 1513 acts are the immediate preparation after Hans's death of 20 February 1513 and predate the coronation of 11 June 1514. Lovkompleks coinage begins in 1514 with the Møntordning af Sommeren 1514 «Dienis Malmø Brev» (Wilcke 7-2 pp. 152-153), which establishes the Hauptkurant Sølvgylden at 8½ pieces/Cologne mark rough at 14 Lod (27.51 g rough / 24.07 g fine). Companion acts of the Lovkompleks: Norge ordinance 3 August 1514 (Kalmar Union extension), Kvittering Paasketid 1515, Sjælland åbent Brev 24 August 1515. Main mint Malmø under mintmaster Diens (Hans) Blicher; Galster c2g-38 attests Sølvgylden 1516+1518+1523 (.875/27.06 g). Skilling pieces 1515-1517 are «betydelig tungere» than the Brev specifies (Wilcke p. 153) — specimen drift above the ordinance soll.

Phase A3 · 1523 → 1523 · Final consolidation 1523 · mass Sølvgylden Malmø pre-deposition 13 April 1523
Last Sølvgylden mass coinage at Malmø on the eve of the deposition. Wilcke 7-2 p. 156 verbatim «meget almindelig» (very common). Reference specimens Bruun-3933 + Bruun-3934 (Bruun lots in the Stack's Bowers Part I auction catalogue) attest specimen brutto 28.74-29.49 g — 4-7 % over the ordinance soll 27.51 g, typical early-modern specimen range. Coinage ends abruptly with Christian II's flight into exile (13 April 1523); Frederik I takes the Danish crown and issues a new Møntordning on 25 February 1524 (8-Gylden-Fod), which tightens the Sølvgylden count to 8/M.
