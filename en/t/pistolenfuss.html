Phase I · 1771 → 1870 · Christian d'or / Frederik d'or · Altona + Copenhagen · 5-Speciedaler tariff
Pistole gold line of the Danish monarchy: Christian d'or in 1771 under Christian VII (Altona, modelled on the Louis d'or[25]); continued under Frederik VI (Frederik d'or, 2 Frederik d'or 1826–1838 despite the Statsbankerot of 1813), Christian VIII (2 Christian d'or 1841–1843), Frederik VII (Frederik d'or, 2 Frederik d'or 1850–1859), Christian IX (Frederik d'or 1869, 2 Christian d'or 1866 / 1869) — last issue 1870. Standard 130/144 fineness (≈ 903 ‰), 6.03 g fine gold, 5-Speciedaler tariff. Demonetised by the Krone reform of 1873 (Scandinavian Monetary Union).
