Flensborg Bestalling of 22 January 1547 under mintmaster Jørgen Kock the Younger[112]. Daler brutto identical to the royal line (29.232 g, 8 pieces per Cologne mark rough), but fineness reduced to 14¼ Lod (890⅝ ‰) ⇒ mf 8.982, 26.035 g fine per Daler (1.73 % lighter than royal). Sub-Mark in Lybsk subdivision: 1 Gylden = 24 ß lybsk = 288 Penning lybsk[113][103] (vs royal 48 ß danske). Main phase 1545-1554; tail through 1571 under Frederik II. Coinage: Rhinsk Gylden, Sølvgylden, Søsling lybsk (Hede c3h15-c3h22.
