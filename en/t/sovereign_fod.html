A brief experimental episode after Christian IV's 1606 England journey: lead piece 1 Sovereign 1608 (Helsingør, Hede 19, ca. 10.0 g, rarity R)[57] on James I's Unite model (22 carats, ca. 10.0 g)[56]; alongside three unique trial variants from Copenhagen — Hede 20 (22.64 g), Hede 21 (8.08-10.75 g), Hede 22 (4.65 g)[58]. Hede classifies the whole 19-22 strand as «Prøvemønt efter engelsk forbillede»; fineness stated in no source, inferred from the English 22-carat Unite. Not carried into mass circulation.
